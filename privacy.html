n8n Agente

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Who this covers

n8n Agente is a private, single-user system operated by Luca Marvelli. Its only user is its owner. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other people.

Google data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the system may use these services, only on the owner's own account:

  • Google Drive and Docs: to create, read and update documents and reports.
  • Google Sheets: to write and read logs and records.
  • Google Slides: to create presentations.
  • Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks: to prepare messages, reminders and tasks for the owner.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to perform the tasks the owner asks for.
  • It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.
  • Parts of a document may be sent to AI model providers when the owner explicitly asks the system to analyse them.
  • Access tokens are stored on infrastructure controlled by the owner.

The system's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Files stay in the owner's Google account, where the owner can delete them at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
