n8n Agente

A private automation system that runs research, reports and monitoring for one person. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it does

  • Collects and compares answers from several AI models on technical questions.
  • Saves the resulting reports as documents in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Keeps logs in the owner's own Google Sheets and checks that its services are working.

Why it uses Google sign-in

The system signs in with the owner's Google account only, to read and write files in that same account. It never accesses anyone else's data.

Details on how data is handled are in the privacy policy.
